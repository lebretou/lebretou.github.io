I am currently a second-year Ph.D. student at University of Maryland, College Park under the guidance of Dr. Fumeng Yang.

Before I joined UMD, I received my Master's degree in Computer Science at Brown University.

At Brown, I worked under Dr. Jeff Huang at Brown HCI Lab. We are exploring the effect of visualizations on people's self-reflection over their personal text data.

To pronounce my name, think of it like "John Jane Shoe". In my free time, I like being a barista and photographer.
