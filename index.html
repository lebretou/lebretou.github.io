I work on visualization and human-computer interaction: designing interfaces, building visualization systems, and exploring novel interactions.

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Dr. Fumeng Yang.

Before UMD, I earned a master's degree in computer science at Brown University. There I worked with Dr. Jeff Huang in the Brown HCI Lab, studying how visualizations affect people's reflection on their personal text data. I also worked with Dr. Emily Wall at Emory University on visual and tactile perception and on how large language models perform visual analytic tasks.

My name sounds roughly like “John Jane Shoe.” In my free time I like being a barista and a photographer.
